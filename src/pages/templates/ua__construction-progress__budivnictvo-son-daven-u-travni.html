Foundations & weight

An architectural field study from the Trapnest Architecture reference collection. These photographs and films are retained from the source experience as observations of structure, material, and landscape.

Looking closely

A building is a sequence of relationships: mass and opening, shelter and horizon, detail and whole. This study invites a slower reading of those relationships, from the scale of a surface to the way a structure sits in its setting.

Reference imagery & film

From reference to experience

Trapnest Architecture uses this visual collection to explore how spaces can be experienced through motion, atmosphere, and interaction. These are architectural references, rather than a construction announcement or an offer of property.
