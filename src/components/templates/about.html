The project

Trapnest Architecture is a spatial chapter of Trapnest, Henry’s journal of Life, Art, Science, and Technology. A place to explore architecture through atmosphere, material, light, and the quiet intelligence of interactive design.

Explore Trapnest

The approach

Stone gives a place its weight. Timber brings warmth. A terrace opens the room to the landscape. Here, architectural imagery and digital craft meet in an experience that invites you to slow down, look closer, and feel the space.
